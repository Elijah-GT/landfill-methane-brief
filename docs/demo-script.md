# Demo video script (2 min 30 s target)

Record at 1280×800 or larger, browser zoom 100%. Use the live site:
https://elijah-gt.github.io/methane-docket/ (press Ctrl+Shift+R once before recording).
The slide deck has speaker notes for every slide; this script interleaves slides and the live app.

Before recording: close other tabs, open `index.html#site=sunshine-canyon-landfill`, scroll to the top, and have the deck open on its cover.

| Time | Screen | Say (roughly) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00 to 0:20 | Deck: cover, then “Methane plumes and the climate” | “Landfills turn food waste into methane. When it escapes, it drifts downwind as a plume that aircraft and satellites can now see. Methane is about 80 times as potent as carbon dioxide, it carries toxic compounds to neighbours, and landfills are California’s second-largest source. Cutting it slows warming this decade.” |

## Do not say

* “Repairs within 3 days.” The regulation does not say that.
* “Violation”, “non-compliant” or “safe”.
* “Satellite data” alone. The data is from aircraft and satellites.
* That a spot is a leak. Say “detections repeat at this spot” and that locations are estimated plume origins.
