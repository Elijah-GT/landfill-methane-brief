What this tool is, and what it is not

It is

  • A history of methane plumes that Carbon Mapper detected near each landfill and published, with dates, rates and uncertainty.
  • A plain-language summary of what California’s amended Landfill Methane Regulation requires once CARB notifies an operator, with every item tied to its section.
  • A per-site companion to CARB’s CalSMP dashboard (opens in a new tab), which shows official notification status.

It is not

  • Legal advice or an official CARB determination. It does not say whether any landfill is meeting the rule.
  • An alert system. Public plume data is released about 30 days after each observation.
  • Proof of the source. A plume near a landfill may come from a neighbouring activity, and every rate carries the uncertainty shown.

Plume data: Source: Carbon Mapper (data.carbonmapper.org), used non-commercially under the Carbon Mapper Terms of Use.

Regulation: California Air Resources Board, Landfill Methane Regulation. Landfill names: U.S. EPA Greenhouse Gas Reporting Program and CalRecycle SWIS.
